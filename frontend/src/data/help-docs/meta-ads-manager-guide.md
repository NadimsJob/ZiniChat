# Meta Ads Manager & 1-Click Page Post Boosting Manual

## Overview
The **Meta Ads Manager** (`মেটা অ্যাডস ম্যানেজার`) empowers ZiniChat merchants to manage connected Facebook Ad Accounts, monitor live ad performance, scale daily budgets by percentage, and boost organic Facebook Page posts in 1 click using native Meta Graph API integration.

---

## 1. Core Feature Pillars

| Feature Pillar | Description | Key Merchant Benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 📊 **Active Campaigns & Scale Hub** | Real-time overview of active campaigns, ad sets, impressions, clicks, CTR, and spend. | Instant status toggle (Active/Paused) and inline daily budget adjustment without opening Meta Ads Manager. |
| 🚀 **1-Click Percentage Scaling** | Quick-scale buttons: `+20%`, `+50%`, `-10%`, `-20%`. | Scale winning campaigns safely by 20% to prevent ad fatigue or budget overspending. |
| ✨ **Boost Existing Page Posts** | Select any photo or status post from the connected Facebook Page and boost it in 1 click. | Preserves 100% of organic likes, comments, and shares using Meta's `object_story_id`. |
| 🛡️ **Zero-MCP Seamless Experience** | Fully automated backend connection via Facebook OAuth. | Merchants never need to configure MCP servers, API keys, or technical tools. |

---

## 2. Connecting Your Facebook Ad Account

1. Navigate to **Dashboard > Marketing & Ads > Meta Ads Manager** (`/dashboard/settings/meta-ads`).
2. Click the blue **"Connect Account"** (`মেটা অ্যাকাউন্ট কানেক্ট করুন`) button.
3. In the Facebook login pop-up, authorize ZiniChat to access your ad accounts (`ads_management`, `ads_read`).
4. Upon redirection, your ad accounts, balance, currency, and connected Facebook Page will be displayed automatically.

---

## 3. Boosting an Existing Facebook Page Post

1. Switch to the **Boost Page Post** (`পেইজ পোস্ট বুস্টিং`) tab.
2. The system automatically fetches recent posts published on your connected Facebook Page.
3. Click **"Boost This Post"** (`এই পোস্টটি বুস্ট করুন`) on your desired post.
4. Set your:
   - **Daily Budget** (e.g., ৳500 or $5)
   - **Duration** (1 to 30 days)
   - **Audience Target** (Country, Age range, Gender)
5. Click **"Launch Ad on Meta Now"** (`এখনই মেটাতে বিজ্ঞাপন লাইভ করুন`). The ad goes live immediately on Meta.

---

## 4. Managing Active Campaigns & 1-Click Budget Scaling

Under the **Active Campaigns & Scale Hub** tab:
* **Pause / Resume**: Click the green/gray toggle switch next to any campaign to pause or resume it immediately.
* **Inline Daily Budget Edit**: Hover over the daily budget and click the pencil icon (`Edit`), enter a new amount, and press Enter or the checkmark (`✓`).
* **1-Click Quick Scaling**:
  - Click **`+20%`** to scale the daily budget by 20%.
  - Click **`+50%`** for aggressive budget scaling.
  - Click **`-10%`** or **`-20%`** to scale down if costs increase.
